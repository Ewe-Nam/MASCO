ABOUT US

Studious & Diligence

A family-centred school where curiosity is celebrated and every child is truly known.

Our Vision

To nurture curiosity and a love of learning in a safe and happy place where strong families thrive and every child is known and valued.

Our Mission

To deliver caring, well-rounded education from Daycare to JHS 3 — building confident, kind and diligent young people, hand in hand with their families.

Our story

Mamfe Apostolic School Complex was founded to give young children a warm, faith-and-values-centred start to their education. What began as a small daycare has grown into a full complex serving families from their earliest years through Junior High — while keeping the close, family feel that has always defined us.

Today our motto, Studious & Diligence, still guides everything we do.
