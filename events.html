RECENT HIGHLIGHTS

A look back at our school year

2026

School Tour — Akosombo & Dodi Island

Our pupils explored the Akosombo Dam and cruised to Dodi Island aboard the Dodi Princess — a memorable day of discovery, fun and friendship.

2024

KG Graduation & Cultural Day

Our youngest graduates celebrated in colourful traditional dress, with performances, drama and proud families cheering them on.

Dec 2023

Christmas Carols Service

Carols, recitals and nativity performances filled the hall with festive joy as pupils shared the Christmas message with their families.

Mar 2024

Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Excursion

A learning trip to Accra's historic landmarks brought history and civics to life for our pupils beyond the classroom.

2024

Science Resource Centre Visit

Hands-on experiments and real laboratory equipment sparked curiosity as our pupils explored science at the resource centre.

2026

ABF Global Mentorship Programme

Our school hosted the ABF Global Mentorship Programme, connecting pupils and staff with mentors to inspire the next generation.

2024

KG Washroom Commissioning

We proudly opened a new, child-friendly washroom facility for our Kindergarten — part of our ongoing investment in a safe, healthy campus.
